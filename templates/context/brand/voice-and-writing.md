## Checklist before handing back

- [ ] Written for one persona from `personas.md`, about their problem
- [ ] The main point is in the first two sentences
- [ ] Every claim traces to `uvp.md` or a product file
- [ ] No word from the "instead of" column
- [ ] Follows the structure for its format
- [ ] Read aloud, it sounds like a person
